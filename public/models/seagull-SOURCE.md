# Seagull

Model, texture, rig, and original procedural animation: svartmc (2020).
Source: https://github.com/svartmc/seagull
Source files: assets/seagull.fbx and assets/seagulltexture.jpg
License: MIT, reproduced in seagull-LICENSE.txt.

Adaptations: FBX converted to glTF, material setup, scale normalization, independent flock rigs, wing and wingtip motion with alternating flapping and gliding. Texture unchanged. Flight animation adapted from the original rig controls.
