# Original painted assets

These project assets were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool for Yixu’s Ocean. No reference-site imagery is included.

- `willow-foliage.png`: 1254×1254 RGBA with genuine transparency. Used as intersecting canopy cards in the 3D world.
- `gouache-paper.png`: 1254×1254 RGB. Used for pigment/color variation and bump detail. Seamless tiling was requested; the generated edges are not mathematically guaranteed seamless.

## Willow prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent game foliage canopy card for a playable 3D moonlit hand-painted storybook ocean world. Generate one original bitmap: a lush weeping willow foliage cluster WITHOUT ANY TRUNK. Multiple fine long drooping tapered leaves form many hanging fronds in a roughly triangular umbrella canopy. Hand-painted gouache and dry-brush printmaking with visible coarse paper speckles inside the paint, broken paint edges, intricate brushwork and organic gaps between leaves. Vivid yellow-green/chartreuse tips, forest emerald and midnight teal shadows. Square composition, complete cluster centered with a little clear space on all sides and dangling ends fully visible. Genuine transparent alpha background throughout exterior and leaf gaps; no painted background, no paper rectangle, no white outline, no trunk, no ground, no scene, no text, no watermark. Fine original artwork, not cartoon clipart. Canopy will repeat on planes in 3D.

## Pigment prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: square seamless tileable surface pigment texture for multiplying onto rocks, plaster, wood and roofs in a hand-painted storybook 3D game. Generate one original bitmap with neutral medium gray-blue gouache pigment texture, fine stippled coarse paper tooth, layered delicate dry brush striations and broken pigment grain. Low contrast, very uniform illumination and density over entire square, soft painterly variation. Flat texture fills whole image edge to edge. Seamless tile: opposite edges match. No subject, no recognizable shapes, no shadows, no directional lighting, no gradients, no vignette, no text, no borders, no frame, no transparent background. Subtle and finely crafted traditional gouache scan, not photographic concrete.
